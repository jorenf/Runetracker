# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be useful and welcoming. Contributors and users are
expected to communicate with respect, assume good intent, and keep feedback
focused on the work.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately derailing issues or pull requests.
- Sharing secrets, tokens, or private guild data.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may block users from
the project when behavior harms the community or the safety of the repository.
